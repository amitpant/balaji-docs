# Product brief

## Goal

A responsive website (desktop and mobile) where Balaji Construction shows its projects to win more clients, plus a private admin panel where the company adds and manages those projects.

**Core rule:** a detail shows on the site only if it was filled in the admin panel. Empty optional fields stay hidden.

## Audience

- **Visitors:** families and businesses in and around Haldwani planning a house, commercial building or interior work. Most arrive on a phone.
- **Admins:** the company owner or staff. Not technical; the admin panel must be simple.

## Scope for v1

### Public website

- **Home:** hero photo with a one-line promise, stats strip, featured projects, services, how we work, testimonials, enquiry form.
- **Projects:** Ongoing / Completed tabs; cards with cover photo, name, location.
- **Project detail:** gallery, videos, and only the details that were filled in.
- **Services:** short cards (residential, commercial, interior, 3D elevation — to confirm).
- **About** and **Contact:** story, address, map, enquiry form.
- **Every page:** floating WhatsApp and call buttons.

### Admin panel (login required)

- **Projects:** add, edit, hide, delete; upload photos, pick the cover, paste YouTube links; featured flag and order.
- **Testimonials:** add, edit, hide.
- **Enquiries:** inbox of form submissions with status (new, contacted, closed).
- **Site settings:** stats numbers, phone, WhatsApp, address, social links.

### Later, not in v1

Blog, careers, media and awards page, FAQs, Hindi version, customer accounts, payments, cost calculator, installable app (PWA).

## Reference site

[psrbuilders.in](https://psrbuilders.in/) is the reference for **content**, not design.

| Feature | Reference site | Our v1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ongoing / Completed projects | Menu items linking to posts | Yes, tabs on the Projects page |
| Project detail | Blog-style post | Yes, structured: gallery, specs, video |
| Stats strip | Projects, years, design plans | Yes, editable in admin |
| Services | Image cards | Yes, simple cards |
| Free consultation form | Name, phone, email, location | Yes, admin inbox + email alert |
| Testimonials | Carousel | Yes, managed in admin |
| How we work | 4-step process | Yes, static section |
| Trust badges | GST, ISO, trademark | Yes, if the client holds them |
| WhatsApp / call | Click-to-call | Floating WhatsApp + call buttons |
| Blog, careers, media, awards | Present | Later |

**Our edge:** every project page shows area, floors, cost (or "on request") and video.

## Open questions for the client

- [ ] Logo, brand colours, exact company name to display
- [ ] How many projects at launch; are photos ready?
- [ ] Show cost publicly, "On request", or per project?
- [ ] Project categories in use
- [ ] Who updates the admin panel; Hindi or English screens?
- [ ] Existing domain or YouTube channel?
- [ ] Phone, WhatsApp, address, enquiry email
- [ ] English only, or English + Hindi later?
- [ ] Monthly budget for domain and hosting
